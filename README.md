# 🚙 Truck & Go‑Bag Preparedness (Non‑Overkill Edition)

This setup is designed for **real‑world inconveniences**, not apocalypse role‑play.
The goal: stay **mobile, calm, warm, fed, and communicating** for 24–48 hours with minimal bulk.

---

## Philosophy

* Prepare for **likely problems**, not every problem imaginable
* Favor **multi‑use items**
* Comfort = better decisions
* Redundancy is intentional, not wasteful

---

## 🚙 Truck Loadout

### Interior (Quick Access)

These live inside the cab so they’re reachable immediately.

* Headlamp (hands‑free lighting)
* LED flashlight (separate from headlamp)
* Work gloves (mechanic or cut‑resistant)
* Compact first aid kit

  * Blister pads
  * Antiseptic wipes
  * Ibuprofen / acetaminophen
  * Medical shears 
* Protein bars / snacks
* Portable lithium battery jumper
* Phone charging cable (short, rugged)
* Tow strap
* Rain jacket / poncho (door pocket)
* Window breaker + seatbelt cutter
* GMRS radio (channels pre‑programmed)
* Small tool kit - wrenches, screw drivers etc.
* Tire pressure gauge
* Micro air compressor (12V)
* Tire plugs/Fix O Flat
* Paper map of local region
* Quality Compass

## 🧰 Truck Bed Tote — Clothing (Per Person)

Designed for unexpected overnight stays or sudden weather changes.

### Clothing

* 3× pairs of socks (wool preferred)
* 3× t‑shirts
* 1× durable pair of pants
* 1x underwear
* Shoes (broken‑in)
* 1x work gloves 
* Weather appropriate hat
* Lightweight hoodie or fleece
** Children's clothes that are appropriate for extended outdoor use if applicable 

**Tip:** Vacuum‑seal clothing to save space and keep items fresh.

### Food

* Sandwich bag of protein bars
* Optional upgrade: jerky or trail mix
*** Three days rations per person is a good base to go off of for portions 

### Miscellaneous/optional

* Soap
* Steel pot (must be sturdy enough to boil water on a camp fire without warping) - Boiling is one of the only ways to truly kill viral pathogens in untreated water
* Water proof bags
* Rope
* Solar charging panel

## 🎒 Grab‑and‑Go Backpack (20–30L)

This bag goes **with you** when the truck cannot.

### Core Essentials

* Water (1–2L bottle or collapsible pouch)
* Water purification method x 2 (filter and tablets)
* Fire starting tools x 2 (Ferro rod, lighter, matches etc.)
* kindling/tinder (cotton, dryer lint, Manila rope) keep sealed, have enough to start several fires and optimally use oil/tar impregnated or soaked products to ensure quick lighting in inclement weather 
* Protein bars / snacks (separate from truck stash) again three days rations is optimal
* Electrolyte drink mix - use more sparingly 
* Vitamins and Minerals - to ensure cognitive clarity and body optimization 
* Compact first aid kit (intentional redundancy) - This kit MUST include any and all things specific to your own and others personal medical needs like - epipens, prescription medications, glass/contacts etc.
* Headlamp
* Flashlight
* Phone battery pack
* GMRS radio or spare battery 
* Light gloves
* Bandana / buff (dust mask, sweat rag, sling, pre‑filter)
* Emergency bivy or space blanket
* Notebook + Sharpie
* Fixed blade knife (for impromptu shelter building and other survival needs)
* Knife/Axe sharpener 
* Rope
* Signaling mirror/panel
* Sunglasses/clearglasses (preferably safety or ballistic rated)
* Basic hygiene kit

  * Toothbrush/toothpaste 
  * Wipes/toilet paper
  * Deodorant
  * Nail trimming and clipping kit 

*** pro tip - storing electronics in Faraday bags/boxes will help to protect them from EMP and is fairly light weight.

### Optional but Smart

* Lightweight multitool - Leatherman,Gerber etc. 
* Earplugs
* Cash ($40–$60, small bills) - I would recommend eventually saving up to $500+ cash is king and can be used to barter for things you may find essential to your well being in emergency situations 
* Quality over ear protection w/sound amplifiers
* Hatchet
* Shovel 
* Folding saw

## 💛 Wife Comfort Pouch (Dedicated Section)

This is a **separate zip pouch** inside the backpack.
It exists to maintain comfort, morale, and calm decision‑making.

### Comfort & Sanity Items

* Lip balm
* Hand lotion (small tube)
* Face wipes / makeup wipes
* Hair ties
* Compact brush or comb
* Travel deodorant
* Sunscreen stick
* Earplugs
* Eye mask
* Hand sanitizer 

### Clothing Comfort Boost

* Extra pair of warm socks
* Soft beanie
* Light scarf or shawl
* Packable down or synthetic jacket
* Thermals

### Personal Care

* Feminine hygiene supplies
* Pain reliever
* Personal medications (clearly labeled)
* Small mirror

### Food & Emotional Stability

* Comfort snack (something genuinely enjoyed)
* Tea bag or instant cocoa
* Electrolyte packets

**Rule:** This pouch is hers. Do not raid it unless invited.

## Why This Works

* Covers mobility, weather, communication, food, and first aid
* Supports both physical needs and emotional comfort
* Avoids unnecessary weight and duplication
* Scales easily by season or family size

Prepared doesn’t have to feel harsh.
Prepared can feel **calm, capable, and considerate**.
